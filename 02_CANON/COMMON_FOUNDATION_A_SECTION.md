## Scope and promotion rule

This document promotes only user-authored decisions and clearly supported normalizations from Questionnaire 1, Section A. AI-generated evaluations, risk analysis, speculative implementation details, and unresolved options are not promoted.

Existing CORE_CANON remains unchanged. This document supplements it.

## Confirmed decisions

### A-1. One-sentence game identity — partial confirmation

Confirmed common identity:
- The game features Hong Gildong fighting enemies originating from the future.
- Combat combines Joseon-era martial/mystical arts (dosul) with heavy, modern-derived firearms reinterpreted for the Joseon setting.
- The larger narrative direction includes establishing Yuldo for the people.

The user's original wording described Yuldo as a Joseon autonomous territory. That political-status wording is not promoted because the existing CORE_CANON states the broad direction as establishing a new country, Yuldo-guk. The final political status is recorded under "Unresolved Canon conflict" below.

### A-2. Distinctive player experience

The distinctive experience has two confirmed pillars:
1. Combat combining Joseon-era dosul and modern firearms.
2. Reinterpreting/localizing modern firearms for Joseon-era technology, circumstances, and context.

The exact extent of localization (appearance, materials, manufacturing, structure, operation, ammunition, or performance) remains open.

### A-3. The setting fusion is a core differentiator, not decoration

The combination of Joseon-era context, modern-derived technology, and dosul is a core differentiating element.

- Modern weapons are reinterpreted and modified in light of Joseon technology and circumstances to create new weapon directions.
- Dosul can strengthen conventional weapons and provide movement and defensive capabilities that compensate for limitations of conventional weapons.

The exact meaning and scope of "conventional weapons," the implementation of weapon enhancement, and the rules, costs, limits, and interactions of dosul remain open for the relevant specialist domains.

### A-4. Hong Gildong, the Hwalbindang, and the emergence of Yuldo

- The story does not begin with Hong Gildong pursuing the construction of Yuldo as his initial ambition.
- As Hong Gildong and the Hwalbindang develop through their actions, people gradually come to follow them.
- The later establishment of Yuldo arises from the responsibility to protect those people and provide them with a place to live.
- The motivation is the protection and welfare of the people, rather than the pursuit of power or territorial expansion.

This supplements, and does not replace, CORE_CANON's broad direction of establishing Yuldo-guk.

### A-5. Priority of player experience

- Combat must be the most enjoyable and primary direct gameplay experience.
- The game also includes a heroic-fantasy narrative centered on Hong Gildong and the Hwalbindang.
- Heroic fantasy is primarily a narrative dimension; it is not intended to have a major direct effect on combat rules or combat performance.
- Because the heroic narrative affects the story, it may also affect game progression, world state, or content to some extent. The specific effects remain open.

The user's stated hope that heroic fantasy may support IP expansion is a design intention/hypothesis, not a confirmed market outcome.

### A-6. Experiences the game does not aim to provide

The game does not aim to be:
1. A realistic military simulation.
2. A game whose core is excessively complex survival or crafting management.
3. A game whose progression is centered on long dialogue and cutscenes rather than combat.

These exclusions do not prohibit weighty combat, weapon handling, meaningful weapon crafting/tuning, or story scenes. Their detailed balance remains open.

### A-7. Weapon creation, tuning, discovery, and presets

- Weapon progression should not consist merely of increasing item grade or changing a border/rarity frame.
- Creating weapons, combining elements, and discovering new combinations are intended to be meaningful sources of enjoyment.
- For some players, weapon creation and tuning may become a major or even primary content activity.
- Players who prefer to move quickly through the story or enter combat without extensive crafting should have weapon presets available.
- Heroic fantasy remains primarily narrative and is not intended to substantially alter combat performance directly, while still being able to affect the story and, through it, other parts of the game.

The exact weapon construction model, component taxonomy, combination rules, preset saving/loading/sharing, and balance between crafted weapons and presets remain open. No particular implementation or numerical balance is confirmed here.

## Unresolved Canon conflict

### Final political status of Yuldo

- Existing confirmed source: CORE_CANON says the broad direction is to establish a new country, Yuldo-guk.
- User answer A-1 described Yuldo as a Joseon autonomous territory.
- A separate Proposed record preserves two possible endings: (A) an autonomous territory within Joseon, and (B) an independent Yuldo-guk.

The autonomous-territory wording and independent-country direction cannot be treated as the same settled political status without an explicit relationship being defined. Therefore:
- The broad CORE_CANON direction remains in force and is not edited.
- The A-1 phrase "autonomous territory" is not promoted as a final political-status decision.
- The two ending possibilities remain Proposed, not Confirmed.
- Whether the autonomous territory is a transitional stage, an alternate ending, or a different meaning of "Yuldo-guk" requires a separate user decision.

## Explicitly not promoted

The following remain open or Proposed:
- Exact weapon recipes, parts, materials, stats, and crafting steps.
- The technical scope of Joseon-style weapon localization.
- Exact dosul abilities, resource costs, restrictions, and activation rules.
- Whether dosul enhancement is attached to weapons, activated separately by the player, or both.
- Exact weapon preset functionality and progression.
- Detailed effects of heroic narrative on progression, world state, and content.
- The number, conditions, and structure of endings.
- Specific plot events, characters, quests, and implementation details.
- AI-generated risk analysis, recommendations, and design hypotheses.
